Good questions

The bits people ask.

Is it really free?
Yes. Dinner ideas and freshness reminders are free forever. Plus (£3.99/month) adds photo scanning, your full fridge/freezer/pantry and the self-building shopping list — with a 14-day trial and no card to start.
Do I need to download it from the App Store?
No. It's a web app — open it in your browser and add it to your home screen in one tap. It then behaves just like a normal app, on both iPhone and Android.
What happens to my photos?
They're read by AI to spot what food is there, then discarded. Nothing is stored and nothing is sold.
How accurate is the scanning?
It reads most items from a clear photo. You get a quick check-list to confirm or tweak before anything is saved, so you're always in control.
Will it work for my diet or allergies?
Yes. Set vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free or allergies once, and every dinner idea respects them.
Can I cancel anytime?
Anytime, in a tap. No lock-in and no awkward emails.
