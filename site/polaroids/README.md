# Polaroid strip images

Drop your lifestyle photos here as **01.jpg … 10.jpg** (portrait, ~4:5,
e.g. 800×1000). They appear in the overlapping polaroid strip above
"How it works" on the landing page.

- **Desktop:** all available images fan out as overlapping, jaunty polaroids.
- **Mobile:** 4 slots cycle through the whole set automatically.

Fewer than 10 is fine — missing slots just show a soft mint placeholder.
Filenames are fixed (zero-padded): 01.jpg, 02.jpg, … 10.jpg.
