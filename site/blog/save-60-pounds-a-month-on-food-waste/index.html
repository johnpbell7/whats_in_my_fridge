Food waste and saving money: frequently asked questions

How much food does the average UK household waste?

A lot. WRAP, the UK waste charity, estimates households throw away millions of tonnes of edible food a year, worth a meaningful chunk of the grocery bill each month. See wrap.org.uk for the latest figures.

How can I save money on food waste?

Buy to a plan, store food well, and cook what is going off first. Most wasted food was perfectly good when it went in and just got forgotten, so tracking what you have and using it in time is where the savings come from.

What foods get wasted the most?

Bread, milk, fresh fruit and vegetables and leftovers top the list in most UK homes, largely because they are perishable and easy to forget. Keeping them visible and cooking them first cuts the waste quickly.
