Diet & allergies

Veggie? Gluten-free? Nut allergy?

What’s in my Fridge · a quick read

Cooking around a diet or an allergy means filtering out half of every recipe site in your head. It shouldn’t be your job to remember every time.

Tell us what works for you — once

Set your diets (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free and more) and anything to avoid (nuts, shellfish, and the rest, plus a free-text note). It lives in your account — you set it once.

Every idea respects it

From then on, every meal idea and answer follows your rules — so you stop scrolling past dinners you can’t eat. Food that actually fits you, without the mental filtering.

Helpful, not a replacement for the label

The app weaves your preferences into every suggestion, but AI can make mistakes — so for allergies especially, always check the label on every product yourself. It’s a smart starting point, not a medical guarantee.

Snap your fridge and get tonight’s dinner in seconds. Free to start — no card needed.
