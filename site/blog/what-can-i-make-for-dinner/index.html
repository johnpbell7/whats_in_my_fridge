What can I make for dinner with what's in my fridge?

9 June 2026 · 6 min read · Updated July 2026

You can almost always build a decent dinner from what is already in the fridge, once you stop hunting for a recipe and use a formula instead. Pick a protein, a veg, a carb and something with flavour, or find your main ingredient in the cheat sheet below and cook the meal it points to. No shop required.

The "shop your fridge" formula

Most weeknight dinners are just four things put together:

Protein + Veg + Carb + Something with flavour

Pick one from each and you have a meal. The "something with flavour" is what stops it tasting like a diet plan: a sauce, a spice mix, a handful of cheese, a squeeze of lemon. For example:

  • Eggs + spinach + bread + cheese → cheesy eggs on toast
  • Chicken + peppers + rice + soy → quick stir-fry
  • Tin of tomatoes + pasta + garlic + cheese → 10-minute pasta
  • Whatever veg is wilting + stock + a tin of beans → throw-it-all-in soup

Cook the things that need using first

Before you decide anything, glance at what is on its way out: the herbs, the half a pepper, the yoghurt near its date, the bag of spinach going limp. Build the meal around those. It is the single best habit for eating well and wasting less, and it is why "what can I make?" and "what needs using up?" are really the same question. Our guide to stopping food waste at home goes deeper, and the fridge storage chart tells you what is genuinely near the end.

Keep a few flavour heroes in: garlic, onions, stock cubes, soy sauce, a hard cheese and dried chilli. They turn "random veg" into an actual dinner.

Backup dinners from the store cupboard

For the nights the fridge really is bare, a small store-cupboard keeps you off the takeaway apps. With a few tins, some pasta or rice, and eggs, you always have a plan B:

  • Pasta with tinned tomatoes, garlic and whatever cheese is in.
  • Egg fried rice from last night's rice and an odd handful of veg.
  • A chickpea curry from a tin, an onion and a spoon of curry paste.
  • Beans on toast, done properly, with cheese and a dash of Worcestershire sauce.

If you often find yourself short of one or two bits, checking a dish against what you have before you cook saves the halfway-through panic.

What to make for dinner: frequently asked questions

What can I make for dinner with what I have?

Build a meal around your main ingredient and a method that suits it. Snap or list what is in your fridge and you get a few real dinners you can make tonight, with any missing bits noted, rather than a shopping trip.

How do I decide dinner without going shopping?

Cook from what you already have. Filtering ideas to no extra shopping turns a full-but-uninspiring fridge into three or four options, which also saves money and waste.

What is a simple formula for dinner?

Protein, plus veg, plus a carb, plus something with flavour. Pick one from each of those four groups and you have a balanced meal. It works with almost anything in the fridge and takes the recipe hunt out of weeknight cooking.

What are some quick dinners from store-cupboard ingredients?

Pasta with tinned tomatoes, egg fried rice, a chickpea curry, or beans on toast done well all come together fast from staples. Keep a few tins, pasta and eggs in and you always have a backup.
