Mark your must-haves

Coffee, milk, butter, eggs, oil, pasta — the things you always want in. Pin them once as your essentials and the app keeps an eye on them for you.

When a pinned essential is running low or gone, it’s flagged so you can add it to the list in time — before the morning it actually matters. No more discovering you’re out at the worst possible moment.

One tap, on the list

See a flagged essential, tap once, and it’s on your shopping list. Never caught short again — the boring restocking handled in the background.
