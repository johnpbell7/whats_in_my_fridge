Keeping essentials stocked: frequently asked questions

How do I keep track of kitchen essentials?

Mark the things you always want in (milk, bread, butter, eggs, oil, coffee) as essentials, then have them flagged the moment you are running low. That way restocking happens before you actually run out.

How do I stop running out of milk and bread?

Pin them as must-haves so you are warned when they are low or gone, and add them to your list in one tap. It removes the mental job of remembering the boring basics every shop.

What are the most common household staples to keep in?

Milk, bread, butter, eggs, cooking oil, pasta or rice, tinned tomatoes, onions and coffee or tea cover most homes. Pinning your own set means you only get reminded about the ones you actually use.
