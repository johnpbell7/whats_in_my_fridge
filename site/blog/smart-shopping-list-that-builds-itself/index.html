Shopping list

Always forget the one thing you went for?

What’s in my Fridge · a quick read

You went for milk. You came back with everything but milk. A list helps — but only if it builds itself and is sorted the way you actually shop.

It builds itself

Every dinner idea you pick quietly adds its missing bits to your list. Run low on a staple and it’s flagged. So the list fills itself from what you’re actually cooking and running out of — you’re not starting from a blank page.

Shop section by section

The list is grouped by aisle — produce, dairy, bakery — so you sweep through the shop in order instead of doubling back. Tick things off as you go.

Tick off, then file it away

Ticked-off items file straight back into your fridge, so there’s no separate “put the shopping away” step in the app. Shop once, and everything’s where it should be.
