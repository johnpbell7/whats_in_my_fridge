Smart shopping lists: frequently asked questions

What is a smart shopping list?

A list that fills itself from what you are actually cooking and running out of, rather than one you write from scratch. Pick a dinner idea and its missing bits are added automatically; run low on a staple and it is flagged.

How do I make a shopping list that sorts by aisle?

Group items by section (produce, dairy, bakery) so you sweep through the shop in order instead of doubling back. A smart list does the grouping for you as items are added.

Can a shopping list update itself?

Yes. As you plan meals and use up staples, the list keeps itself current. Tick items off at the shop and they can file straight back into your fridge, so there is no separate put-away step.
