# 📱 First 9 social posts — launch sequence

A sequence, not 9 random posts: **problem → magic → value → proof → ask**.
Pairs with `LAUNCH.md`. App: https://app.whatsinmyfridge.co.uk

## Three rules that beat any single post
1. **Hook in the first 1.5 seconds** — text on screen + a visual that makes
   people stop scrolling. Most of performance is the first frame/line.
2. **Vertical 9:16, show the real screen.** The scan-to-list moment is the whole
   pitch — let people *see* it happen.
3. **Soft CTA, reply to every comment fast.** Early reach comes from engagement,
   not the post. Answer every comment/DM quickly.

---

## The posts

### 1 — Origin story (the problem) · talking-head / text-over-b-roll
- **Hook:** "I was throwing away £60 of food a month without realising."
- **Show:** opening the fridge, finding something gone off → cut to the app.
- **Say:** the honest problem (duplicates, forgetting, binning it). "So I built
  a thing to fix it." No hard sell.
- **Caption:** short story + "Free to try, link in bio."

### 2 — The magic moment (hero post) · screen-record, ~15s
- **Hook:** "Watch AI log my entire fridge from one photo."
- **Show:** snap a messy fridge → items auto-populate. Let it breathe.
- The post to repost/boost most. Make the scan moment crisp.
- **Caption:** "No typing. Snap → it's logged. #foodwaste"

### 3 — "What can I make for dinner?" · screen-record
- **Hook:** "I asked my fridge what's for dinner."
- **Show:** chat → a real meal from what's in there → add extras to the list in
  one tap.
- **Say:** the "staring into the fridge with no idea" feeling — solved.

### 4 — Educational / myth-bust · carousel or text-over-video listicle
- **Hook:** "How long your food *actually* lasts (you bin it too early)."
- **Show:** 5–6 quick facts (milk, eggs, chicken, leftovers, herbs). Ties to the
  blog post → SEO + saves.
- Built to be **saved & shared**, which the algorithm rewards.

### 5 — Relatable skit / meme · short, funny, no product for first 3s
- **Hook:** "POV: you buy milk you already had 3 of."
- **Show:** the pain acted out → reveal the app as the punchline/fix.
- Lightest post — built for shares, not conversions.

### 6 — Receipt scan demo · screen-record
- **Hook:** "Logged my whole weekly shop in 4 seconds."
- **Show:** photograph a till receipt → everything logs at once.
- **Say:** kills the "I'm not typing 30 items in" objection.

### 7 — Money / stats angle · stat-led, text-on-screen
- **Hook:** "UK homes bin ~£700 of food a year. Here's how I cut mine."
- **Show:** the freshness warnings + "use this soon" nudges.
- **Say:** cost-of-living relevance — saves money, not just food.

### 8 — Behind the scenes / social proof · authentic, lo-fi
- **Hook:** "I built this in my kitchen — here's what people are saying."
- **Show:** early user reactions/screenshots, or you using it day-to-day.
- Use real quotes from your 3–5 testers. Builds trust before the ask.

### 9 — Direct CTA / offer · clean, simple
- **Hook:** "It's free. Here's how to start in 10 seconds."
- **Show:** open app → add to home screen → first scan. Mention the 14-day Plus
  trial, no card.
- **Ask:** "Try it free — link in bio."

- **Week 2:** posts 4–6 (value + more demos).

## What to measure (Vercel Analytics)
Watch the funnel: pageviews → `signup` → `upgrade_started` → `subscribed`.
- Low signups → the landing page / first scan needs work.
- Signups fine, upgrades low → the value of Plus / where the prompt appears.
